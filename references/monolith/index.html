The studio

Eleven people, one long table, and a belief that a building should feel inevitable — as if it had always been there.

0Built works
0Countries
0Awards

  1. Site before form

    We spend the first month on the land: sun paths, wind, sound, the walk to the door.

  2. Few materials, honestly used

    Two or three materials per building, left exposed, chosen to age well rather than stay new.

  3. Light as structure

    Openings are drawn first. Walls are what is left between them.

  4. Built to be repaired

    Every detail can be reached, replaced and understood by a local builder.
